How GIFTERA works.

Two simple ways to find a gift that feels right.

🎁

Share something lovely

List a genuine, unused gift with clear photos and details. Our team reviews each listing.

🔁

Choose your kind of exchange

Swap directly with someone who wants your gift, or use Gifter Points to claim another approved gift.

🚚

Confirm and send

Agree to the exchange and delivery charges. Courier details and tracking are shared with both sides.

What are Gifter Points?

Gifter Points are proposed, non-cash platform credits used to claim approved gifts. They cannot be withdrawn or transferred. In the live service, points are awarded only after a gift is verified and successfully exchanged, subject to the final terms.

This prototype does not issue real points, take payments, book couriers or create binding exchanges.
